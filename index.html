    Three ideas behind the look

    Institutional gravitas

    Oxford Blue anchors cover banners, section headings and table headers, so every page reads as enduring and academic.

    High-contrast scannability

    Keep 65% of every page white, set body at 1.18 line spacing and right-align currency, so executives can scan budgets and gates in seconds.

    Cross-platform survival

    Georgia and Arial with plain inline tables avoid broken text boxes and font swaps when drafts move between Canva, Google Docs, Word and PDF.

      Apply it in Canva

      Style once, copy everywhere

      Format one text box per role. Then use Copy style (Ctrl or Cmd + Alt + C) and Paste style (Ctrl or Cmd + Alt + V) on every other heading or paragraph.

      Line spacing

      Open Spacing in the top toolbar and type the line spacing from the specs. Body is 1.18, table text 1.12, title 1.08.

      If a number looks off

      Canva's size units can differ from points. Check one heading against the page above at 100% zoom and keep the ratios: 22 : 15.5 : 12 : 10 : 8.5.

      Three marks, three places.

      The horizontal FU Keystone and wordmark lockup goes on covers and running headers. The circular Concentric Crest Seal goes beside formal sign-offs. Pick a mark, then test your size.

      Drawn to A4 scale. The marks are placeholders: insert your approved files at the same size. The dashed line is the clear space. [TBD] means the guide does not specify it.

      px assumes an A4 page 794 px wide (96 dpi). If Canva shows millimetres, use the slider.

      Three rules for every placement

      Keep the clear space

      Leave empty room equal to 0.5x the emblem height on every side. Measure the emblem on your own file.

      Lock the aspect ratio

      Resize from a corner handle only. In Canva, never drag a side handle: it stretches or crops the mark.

      No effects, ever

      No drop shadows, outer glows, borders or unapproved bright blue fills. Try each mistake below.

      Contrast of the brand pairs

      Calculated with the WCAG contrast formula. Oak on white is too faint for text, so keep it to rules and borders.

      Approved standards

      • Oxford Blue, Pure White and European Oak as the brand focus
      • Georgia headings and Arial 10 pt body at 1.18 line spacing
      • Left-aligned prose with #F4F6F9 callout boxes
      • Right-aligned Euro currency figures in tables

      Banned formatting habits

      • Default bright blue (#4472C4 or #1155CC) or orange table themes
      • Unformatted walls of 11 pt Calibri, or justified text with wide gaps
      • Stretched logos, 3D shadows or neon text highlights
      • Em dashes. Use periods, commas, colons or standard hyphens.
